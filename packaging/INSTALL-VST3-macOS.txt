Vox Smoove (VST3) - installing on a Mac for FL Studio
=====================================================

Works on macOS 10.14 or newer, on Apple Silicon (M1 and later) and Intel Macs.

This build isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS needs one extra step.

1. Copy "Vox Smoove.vst3" into your VST3 folder. In Finder press Cmd+Shift+G, paste this
   path and press Return, then drag the file in:

     ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

2. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities), paste this line and press Return:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Vox Smoove.vst3"

   It removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag. Without it macOS says the plug-in
   is damaged or can't be opened. No output means it worked.

3. In FL Studio: Options > Manage plugins > Find more plugins (or Find installed plugins).
   Vox Smoove (Playbox Audio) then shows up under Plugin database > Effects, and in the
   mixer's effect slots.

To pick a colour theme, click the gear at the top right of the plug-in.
