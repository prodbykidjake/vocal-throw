Vox Smoove - installing on your Mac (Apple Silicon)
====================================================

These builds are not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so macOS needs one extra step.

1. Copy the plug-ins into place (in Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and paste each folder path):

     Vox Smoove.component  ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
     Vox Smoove.vst3       ->  ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/

2. Open Terminal and paste this (one line), then press Return:

     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Vox Smoove.component" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Vox Smoove.vst3"

   This removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag. Without it macOS says the plug-in
   is damaged or can't be opened.

3. Restart your DAW (or rescan plug-ins). The plug-in is listed under Playbox Audio.
   In Logic: Audio FX > Audio Units > Playbox Audio > Vox Smoove.

If Logic doesn't show it, quit Logic and run in Terminal:
     killall -9 AudioComponentRegistrar
then open Logic again (it rescans Audio Units).
