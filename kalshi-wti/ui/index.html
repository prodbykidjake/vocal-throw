      How to read this
      
      
        
        
      • Target is the WTI price when the window opened. Up pays if the price at the close is at or above it.
        • 
        
      • Model = chance the price ends above target given the gap, time left and recent volatility, nudged by what it has learned from past windows.
        • 
        
      • Edge = model chance − contract price − Kalshi fee. The coach only says BUY when the edge clears your minimum.
        • 
        
      • WAIT is the normal state. The line under it says exactly what would turn it into a trade.
        • 
        
      • Paper trades are what the coach would have done. Compare them with your own before trusting it.
